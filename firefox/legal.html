CacheBuddy

Privacy, terms & permissions

Effective November 4, 2026 · CacheBuddy 1.1.0 · Made by Yap

What stays on your device

CacheBuddy reads your active tab’s address when you open its popup. When you choose Cache, Check for changes, or Update, it processes the selected page or public pages on the selected site. A page snapshot may include private or account information in the current page, including content present in its markup. The extension stores saved content, original URLs, titles, dates, size information, and content hashes in your Firefox profile. It also stores your theme preference locally.

CacheBuddy listens for failed top-level web navigations and looks for a matching saved address. The extension does not record a browsing history. Firefox may retain your visits to original addresses and the local viewer in its usual history. CacheBuddy has no developer-operated backend, analytics, advertising, or account system. It does not sell or transmit your saved content to the developer or to data brokers.

Network requests and external links

Saving or checking can send requests to the selected site for pages, resources, and robots.txt. These extension requests omit cookies and authentication credentials. The site can still see ordinary request information such as your IP address. Contact, Donate, and GitHub links open their respective services or your mail app only when you choose them. Those services handle information under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Copies stay in your local Firefox profile until you delete them from Saved or remove the extension. Checking for changes does not replace a saved archive. Your saved copies may be visible to anyone with access to this Firefox profile.

Permitted use and third-party rights

Save and use content only where you have permission or another lawful basis. Respect applicable copyright and privacy law, publisher terms, and any access restrictions. A saved copy does not grant a license to reproduce, distribute, or republish third-party content. CacheBuddy is independent of the sites it can save and is not affiliated with Google. Third-party names and content belong to their respective owners.

Copies are snapshots. They can be incomplete or outdated, and interactive features may be unavailable. CacheBuddy is provided as available. Applicable consumer and statutory rights remain in effect.

Firefox permissions

  • Active tab and scripting. Read the selected tab and capture its rendered HTML after a page action.
  • Site access. Requested for the selected host when you save or check. Used to fetch pages and assets from the selected origin.
  • Web navigation. Detect a failed navigation and open a matching offline copy.
  • Background page. Parse and sanitize HTML and finish the requested job after the popup closes.
  • Storage. Save preferences locally. Expanded storage supports archived pages and their images, styles and fonts.
